Privacy Policy — Clone to My Drive

Last updated: 2026-11-07

Clone to My Drive is a Chrome extension that copies Google Drive items you select into a destination folder on your Google account.

What data we process

Data Where it stays Why
Google sign-in token Managed by Chrome; not on the developer’s servers Your Google account — Drive access when you click Copy
Destination folder URL, UI language, copy options On your device only (chrome.storage.local) Remembers your preferences
URL of the active tab when you open the app Read once per open; not sent to our servers List items to copy from that Drive page
File/folder names, IDs, types in the list From Google Drive API; shown in the app Let you choose what to copy

One OAuth application is published by the extension author; that does not give the author access to your Drive. Only you approve access to your account.

We do not run our own backend, sell data, or use analytics trackers.

Third parties

Only Google: sign-in via Chrome and the Google Drive API. See Google Privacy Policy.

Data sharing

We do not share your data except with Google to perform copies you request.

Retention and deletion

Local preferences remain until you clear extension data or uninstall. Revoke access at Google Account → Third-party access.
